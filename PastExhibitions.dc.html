Calder at SAM

A new Calder exhibition opens each year, downtown and at the Olympic Sculpture Park.

Past exhibition

Following Space: Thaddeus Mosley & Alexander Calder

November 20, 2024–June 1, 2025

Seventeen monumental wood sculptures by Thaddeus Mosley, made between 2001 and 2024, shown alongside five works by Calder in SAM's double-height galleries. It was the first exhibition to bring the two artists together.

Past exhibition

Selections from the Shirley Family Calder Collection

November 20, 2024–June 1, 2025

A focused gallery drawn from the more than 45 Calder works given to SAM by Jon and Kim Shirley, a collection built over 35 years.

Past exhibition

Calder, in Motion: The Shirley Family Collection

November 8, 2023–October 20, 2024

The inaugural Calder exhibition at SAM, tracing his career from the 1920s to the 1970s through mobiles, stabiles, paintings, prints and illustrations.
